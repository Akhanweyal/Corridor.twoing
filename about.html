About Corridor Towing

A local, licensed and insured towing company in Henrico, Virginia.

Who we are

Corridor Towing is a local towing and roadside assistance company based at 8607 Oakview Avenue in Henrico, VA. We run our own trucks and drivers, and we answer the phone ourselves — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays.

We tow cars, SUVs, pickups, vans and box trucks, and we handle jumpstarts, lockouts, tire changes, fuel delivery and winch outs across Richmond, Henrico, Chesterfield, Glen Allen, Short Pump, Mechanicsville and Midlothian.

What you can count on

  • Licensed & insured. Your vehicle is covered while it is in our care.
  • Prices up front. A $70 car or $80 truck pickup fee, $2 per enroute mile and $6 per loaded mile — the request form shows your estimate before you send it. See all prices.
  • Live tracking. Every online request gets a link to follow your driver on a map.
  • Receipt every time. Pay on arrival or online by Venmo, Cash App, PayPal or card.
