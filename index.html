Towing & Roadside Help Across the Richmond Area

Corridor Towing is a licensed and insured towing company based at 8607 Oakview Avenue in Henrico, Virginia. We tow cars, SUVs, trucks and vans and handle jumpstarts, lockouts, tire changes, fuel delivery and winch outs — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Areas we serve: Richmond, Henrico, Chesterfield, Glen Allen, Short Pump, Mechanicsville, Midlothian and surrounding areas within about 30 miles of Henrico.

How much does a tow cost in Richmond, VA?

A car or SUV tow is a $70 pickup fee, plus $2 per mile for the drive from our shop in Henrico to you (enroute) and $6 per mile while your vehicle is on the truck. A full-size truck or van tow is an $80 pickup fee with the same mileage rates. There are no free miles and no hidden fees, and the online request form shows your price estimate before you submit.

Is Corridor Towing open 24 hours?

Yes. Corridor Towing is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. Call or text (804) 292-8414 any time.

What areas does Corridor Towing serve?

We serve Richmond, Henrico, Chesterfield, Glen Allen, Short Pump, Mechanicsville, Midlothian and the surrounding areas, roughly within 30 miles of our shop at 8607 Oakview Avenue, Henrico, VA 23228.

How fast can a tow truck get to me?

Our average response time is 30 to 45 minutes across the Richmond metro area. After you request service you get a link to follow your driver on a live map.
