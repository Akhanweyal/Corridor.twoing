Our Services & Pricing

How to choose: Light Duty for most passenger cars and small SUVs. Medium Duty for larger vehicles like full-size trucks, vans, box trucks, or RVs.

Light Duty Tow

$60 base
  • Pickup: $60
  • Enroute: $2/mile
  • Tow (loaded): $5/mile
  • Most sedans, SUVs, small trucks
  • Examples: Corolla, Civic, Camry
  • Up to 10,000 lbs

Medium Duty Tow

$70 base
  • Pickup: $70
  • Enroute: $2/mile
  • Tow (loaded): $5/mile

  • Examples: F-150, Transit, Sprinter
  • Up to 26,000 lbs
