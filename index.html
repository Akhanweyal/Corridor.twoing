Our Services & Pricing

Not sure which one you need? If it's a regular car, SUV, or small truck, pick Car & SUV Tow. Something bigger — full-size truck, van, or RV — pick Truck & Van Tow. Everything else just needs Roadside Help.

Car & SUV Tow

Also called "Light Duty"

$60 base
  • Pickup fee: $60
  • Enroute: $2/mile
  • Tow (loaded): $5/mile
