Towing Questions & Answers

Prices, hours, service area and how Corridor Towing works in Richmond, Henrico and Chesterfield, VA.

How much does a tow cost in Richmond, VA?

A car or SUV tow is a $70 pickup fee, plus $2 per mile for the drive from our shop in Henrico to you (enroute) and $6 per mile while your vehicle is on the truck. A full-size truck or van tow is an $80 pickup fee with the same mileage rates. There are no free miles and no hidden fees, and the online request form shows your price estimate before you submit.

Is Corridor Towing open 24 hours?

Yes. Corridor Towing is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. Call or text (804) 292-8414 any time.

What areas does Corridor Towing serve?

We serve Richmond, Henrico, Chesterfield, Glen Allen, Short Pump, Mechanicsville, Midlothian and the surrounding areas, roughly within 30 miles of our shop at 8607 Oakview Avenue, Henrico, VA 23228.

How fast can a tow truck get to me?

Our average response time is 30 to 45 minutes across the Richmond metro area. After you request service you get a link to follow your driver on a live map.

How much is a jumpstart, lockout or tire change?

Jumpstarts, lockouts, tire changes and winch outs are $60 flat each. Fuel delivery is $60 plus the cost of the fuel. For a tire change you supply the spare.

What kinds of vehicles can you tow?

Cars, SUVs and small trucks up to 10,000 lbs (light duty), and full-size trucks, vans, box trucks and RVs up to 26,000 lbs (medium duty). We use flatbed and wheel-lift trucks.

How do I request a tow?

Call (804) 292-8414, or fill in the online request form at corridortowing.org/request.html. Tell us where the vehicle is, where it is going, and what you drive. We call to confirm and send a driver.

How can I pay?

You can pay the driver on arrival or pay online with Venmo, Cash App, PayPal or a card. A receipt is sent when the job is done.

Is Corridor Towing licensed and insured?

Yes. Corridor Towing is fully licensed and insured.

My car will not start or is stuck in park. Can you still tow it?

Yes. Tell us when you request service whether the vehicle starts and whether it can shift into neutral, so we send the right truck.
